Background images (all public domain / CC0, from Wikimedia Commons)

The .webp files served by the app are lossy re-encodes (WebP, quality 75,
Pillow method 6) of the original JPEGs, converted to sRGB. The untouched
originals are kept in assets/backgrounds/original/ for reference.
